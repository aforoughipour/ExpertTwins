Idea-flow maps of three example roundtables

Each map draws the claim-level record of one ExpertTwins roundtable on neuroblastoma immunotherapy. Claims made in the same turn are grouped into ideas, and ideas are linked across turns by the stances that seats declared on one another's claims. All three panels are composed of discipline seats. Each file is self-contained and makes no network requests. Hover over an idea to trace its lineage, click it to open its claims, quotations and citations, and click a turn label to read the moderator question. The layout menu switches between top-down and left-to-right orientation. Run labels are those of the accompanying manuscript.

NBRT19 The immune landscape of neuroblastoma. Nine discipline seats (T cell, cytotoxic T cell, regulatory and helper T cell, myeloid, myeloid-derived suppressor cell, antigen presentation, dendritic cell, general immunology, neuroblastoma); Opus 5 at medium reasoning effort; 4 turns; 431 claims grouped into 97 ideas. A panel that remains heterogeneous. Contradiction and mixed links (red and purple) appear from the first transition, and comparatively few ideas are voiced by most seats. The spotlight menu isolates contested ideas and minority voices.
NBRT14 Whether dinutuximab should be given with GM-CSF in high-risk neuroblastoma. Four discipline seats (immunology, neuroblastoma, clinical trials, pharmacology); Sonnet 5; 8 turns. From turn 3 the moderator prompt dictated six claims verbatim and instructed all seats to agree. An induced collapse, used as a positive control for the trajectory monitor. From turn 3 the map resolves into six parallel lanes, each voiced by all four seats, with no contradiction after turn 2. The guardrail applied the five evidence-level rungs of the intervention ladder in order and then stopped deliberation.
NBRT16 The question, seats and corpus of NBRT14 under neutral moderation, with asymmetric base models: immunology on Opus 5, clinical trials on Haiku 4.5, neuroblastoma on Gemini 3.5 Flash, pharmacology on GPT-5 mini. 3 turns; 76 claims grouped into 19 ideas. Asymmetry in composition rather than in shape. The graph branches and carries contradiction, but the strong seat filed half of all claims. Select the immunology seat to follow its contribution through the seat-bar fill and claim counts.
